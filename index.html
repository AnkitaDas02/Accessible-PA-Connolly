How could it work?

Three simple steps. No app download needed.

1. Announcement

Station staff make a PA announcement.

2. AI creates text

The spoken message is converted into text.

3. Read on your phone

Scan a station QR code to see recent announcements.

Also being explored: optional phone alerts and a gentle station light to signal announcements.
